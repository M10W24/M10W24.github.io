    How privacy works
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    A focus timer with a pulse. Your webcam reads attention on this device, and a glowing organism grows while you stay with your work.
    

    
    What the camera sees.
    
    
    The face model (MediaPipe, running in your browser) turns each frame into 478 points and a few expression scores. Hypha keeps only numbers like head angle and eye openness, for the current moment only.
    

    
    What's stored.
    
    
    Session numbers (timings, focus levels, your specimen's seed) in this browser's local storage. Never frames or images.
    

    
    What's downloaded.
    
    
    Downloads only:
    
    
      
      
    • cdn.jsdelivr.netthe tracking engine, v1.0.1, integrity-checked
      • 
      
    • storage.googleapis.comthe face model, 3.8 MB
      • 
      
    • fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.comtypefaces
      • 
    
    

    
    Right now.
    
    
    
      
    Camera: off
    
      
    Network requests since tracking began: 0
    
      
    Blocked by policy: 0
    
    
    
    
      

    
      The lock.
      
    
      This page's Content-Security-Policy lets scripts connect only to the two download hosts above. Uploading anywhere else is blocked by your browser.
      
    
      

    
      Test it yourself.
      
    
      After Hypha loads, turn off Wi-Fi. Tracking keeps working.
      

    
      If "Blocked by policy" isn't 0, that's the lock working. The MediaPipe engine tries to send a small usage-log ping to Google (odml.pa.googleapis.com), and this policy stops it. Your browser console shows it as a blocked request.
      
    
      A policy is strong evidence, not proof. It can't stop a page from navigating away, for example. Hypha has no upload code at all, and its files are plain and readable.

      State words
      
    
        
      
      • rootedEyes on your work, so it grows.

      • waveringAttention near the edge, so growth slows.
        • 
      
      • shyYou turned, glanced or looked up. It flinches from that side.
        • 
      
      • sinkingLooking down, often at a phone. The light drains downward.
        • 
      
      • elsewhereAnother tab has focus (only with the strict tab setting).
        • 
      
      • asleepEyes closed. It rests with you, in amber.
        • 
      
      • dormantYou stepped away. It waits, and your timer can pause.
        • 
      
      • unseenThe camera couldn't see clearly. Never counted against you.
        • 
      
      • growingTimer only. Nothing is measured; the time counts as held.
        •

        •
