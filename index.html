How it works: a face-tracking model (MediaPipe) finds 478 points on your face. The page compares your head angle to a calibration taken when you first start, reads eye-direction and blink signals, and checks whether this tab is visible. Look at the screen for the first 2 seconds so it can calibrate. Works best with even lighting and your face fully in frame. Camera access requires the page to be served over https or opened from localhost/file.
