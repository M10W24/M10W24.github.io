Focus Tracker

    

      

      

        
01 · Welcome

        
A focus timer that notices when you drift.

        
Focus Tracker is a Pomodoro-style timer that uses your webcam to tell when you're looking at your work. It counts your focused time, notices when you look away or check your phone, and shows you how each session went.

      


      

        
02 · Privacy

        
The camera sees 478 dots, not you.

        
Face tracking runs entirely in this tab. No video or images are recorded, stored, or sent: only focus numbers, kept on this device. Try it: once the page has loaded, go offline. It keeps working.

    How privacy works
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    A focus timer that uses your webcam, on this device only, to measure how much of each session you spend focused.
    

    
    What the camera sees.
    
    
    The face model (MediaPipe, running in your browser) turns each frame into 478 points and a few expression scores. The app keeps only numbers like head angle and eye openness, for the current moment only.
    

    
    What's stored.
    
    
    Session numbers (timings and focus levels) in this browser's local storage. Never frames or images.
    

    
    What's downloaded.
    
    
    Downloads only:
    
    
      
      
    • cdn.jsdelivr.netthe tracking engine, v1.0.1, integrity-checked
      • 
      
    • storage.googleapis.comthe face model, 3.8 MB
      • 
      
    • fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.comtypefaces
      • 
    
    

    
    Right now.
    
    
    
      
    Camera: off
    
      
    Network requests since tracking began: 0
    
      
    Blocked by policy: 0
    
    
    
    
      

    
      The lock.
      
    
      This page's Content-Security-Policy lets scripts connect only to the two download hosts above. Uploading anywhere else is blocked by your browser.
      
    
      

    
      Test it yourself.
      
    
      After the page loads, turn off Wi-Fi. Tracking keeps working.
      

    
      If "Blocked by policy" isn't 0, that's the lock working. The MediaPipe engine tries to send a small usage-log ping to Google (odml.pa.googleapis.com), and this policy stops it. Your browser console shows it as a blocked request.
      
    
      A policy is strong evidence, not proof. It can't stop a page from navigating away, for example. This app has no upload code at all, and its files are plain and readable.

      States
      
    
        
      
      • focusedEyes on your work. Focused time counts up.
        • 
      
      • driftingYour attention is near the edge of the screen.
        • 
      
      • looking awayYou turned, glanced aside or looked up.
        • 
      
      • looking downLooking down, often at a phone.
        • 
      
      • other tabAnother tab has focus (only with the strict tab setting).
        • 
      
      • eyes closedEyes closed. Short pauses to think are fine.
        • 
      
      • awayYou stepped away. The timer can pause until you're back.
        • 
      
      • not visibleThe camera couldn't see clearly. Never counted against you.
        • 
      
      • timer onlyNo camera. Nothing is measured; all the time counts as focused.
        • 
      
      • breakA break between rounds.
        • 
      
      • pausedPaused. Nothing is counted.
        • 
    
      

    
      Stats
      
    
        
      
      • DistractionLooking away, down or at another tab for more than a moment.
        • 
      
      • RefocusComing back to your work after a distraction.
        • 
      
      • RecoveredA distraction followed by 60 s of steady focus.
        • 
      
      • StreakUnbroken focused time. Glances under 5 s don't break it.
        • 
    
      
    
      Drifts you mark as fine (F, “That was fine”) are never counted.
